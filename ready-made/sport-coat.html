The cloth

Every thread is uneven by design. The slubs catch the light differently from one panel to the next, so no two coats look quite alike.

The lapel

Rolled, not pressed flat. A line of hand pick-stitching holds the edge, and the buttonhole is cut open for a flower.

The buttons

Real horn, each with its own grain and tone, sewn on with a shank so the front closes cleanly.

The pocket

A curved barchetta breast pocket, shaped like the hull of a small boat, follows the line of the chest.
