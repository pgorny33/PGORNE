P.GÓRNE Menu

Made to Measure at P.GÓRNE extends beyond body measurements. We consider your preferences and the occasions you dress for, then build in the allowances that let each garment drape properly, cut from a pattern that is yours alone.

The Offering

Tailoring

Jackets, suiting, and formalwear, cut with a soft shoulder and a high armhole for comfort and movement.

Trousers & Denim

Five-pocket trousers fitted through the waist, hips, and rise, where every body differs and ready-to-wear cannot follow. In denim, twill, corduroy, linen, and cashmere.

Knitwear

Silk-cashmere shirts and polos, cashmere sweaters and cardigans.

Outerwear

Wool and cashmere overcoats, and rainproof casual jackets.

The Cloth

Loro Piana, Holland & Sherry, Dormeuil, Scabal, Liberty London, Albini, Alumo, Candiani, and more. Natural fibers with character and drape: denim of every kind, twills in over one hundred colors, corduroy, linen, and cashmere.

The Making

Our five-pocket trousers are cut and sewn in New York by a single maker. Tailoring, knitwear, and outerwear are entrusted to specialists in each discipline, at home and abroad.

Perfection takes time. Fittings, alterations, and repairs are part of the process; we refine each garment with you until your one-of-one pattern is set, and it is kept as the foundation for everything that follows.
