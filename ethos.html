P.GÓRNE Return

P.GÓRNE is a New York City atelier offering a wide range of garments, each crafted by a specialist in its respective discipline. We partner with the world's finest cloth mills and artisans to ensure precision and uphold the highest standard of quality. The atelier approaches every garment with an unwavering commitment to proportion, craftsmanship, cloth, and longevity.

Proportion

Intentional proportions create balance, stature, and a fit that can only be achieved through experienced pattern making.

Craftsmanship

Each garment is crafted with a level of precision and attention to detail that can only be achieved by makers whose skill has been refined through years of experience and discipline.

Cloth

The world's finest cloth mills produce natural fibers with character, performance, and a quiet luxury that cannot be replicated through mass production.

Longevity

Longevity is the natural result of proportion, craftsmanship, and cloth. Our garments are designed to be lived in, create memories in, and improve with time.
